Work

The work is usually where the questions become tangible.

Over the years, I have worked with startups, companies, institutions, consultancies, and teams building things across different industries and markets.

Some needed a clearer product. Some needed a better way to explain what they did. Some had useful technology but struggled to communicate its value. Others were dealing with complicated information, new markets, or systems that had grown harder to manage.

My role has changed from project to project.

Sometimes I have been brought in to shape the strategy. Sometimes to design the product or brand. Sometimes to research the problem, build the thing, or help a team work through a decision.

Most of the work sits somewhere between those things.

Selected work

Making sustainability information useful

Sustainability journalism and intelligence

I co-publish and help lead a research driven platform focused on sustainability across Africa.

The challenge is familiar in this field. There is no shortage of research, reports, data, or commentary. The harder part is helping people understand what matters, why it matters, and what the information means in context.

My work spans editorial direction, research systems, digital product, data storytelling, publishing workflows, partnerships, and the wider experience of the platform.

Making an energy consultancy easier to understand

Energy transition and market intelligence

An energy consultancy had strong technical knowledge but needed a clearer way to communicate its expertise, research, and point of view.

I worked across research, thought leadership, communications, and digital presentation, translating developments in energy markets and the transition to cleaner systems into useful material for clients and wider audiences.

The work sat between research and communication. The goal was not to make technical work simpler than it was, but to make it easier to understand.

Turning field data into a usable system

Data collection and infrastructure

A field data company had built substantial infrastructure for collecting information through a large network of agents.

The technology was capable, but the value of the system was not always obvious from the outside.

I worked across brand, positioning, product experience, and digital communication to make the underlying capability easier to understand and trust.

The work reinforced something I have seen repeatedly: good technology still needs a clear story.

Making industrial procurement less difficult

B2B procurement marketplace

Industrial procurement is full of information, but more information does not necessarily make a decision easier.

I worked on a large B2B procurement marketplace, using research to understand where buyers were getting stuck and then reworking parts of the product experience around how they actually searched, evaluated, and purchased products.

The commercial requirements mattered just as much as the interface. The work was about finding a better balance between what buyers needed and what the business needed to achieve.

Building trust into a document heavy service

Translation technology

Certified translation involves documents that can affect visas, contracts, applications, and other important decisions.

I worked on the customer experience across these journeys, looking at where people became uncertain, where opportunities to use the service were being missed, and how product experience could better support acquisition and retention.

The interesting part was the relationship between trust and conversion. When someone is already dealing with an important document, even a small point of confusion can be enough to make them stop.

Explaining a different kind of travel marketplace

Cooperative and community focused travel

A travel platform was built around a different model from the conventional holiday rental marketplace. A portion of booking activity supported local community projects.

The challenge was communicating that model without turning the product into a lecture.

I worked across the brand story, booking experience, and local activity journeys, helping make the relationship between the traveller, the platform, and the communities involved easier to understand.

Designing trust for high value commerce

B2B electronics marketplace

I joined a growing wholesale electronics marketplace as its first full time designer.

The products were valuable, transactions were often between people who had never worked together, and trust was therefore part of the product itself.

I built the design function across the product, seller tools, analytics, and visual identity, creating a more consistent experience around buying and selling high value devices.

The work taught me that sometimes the biggest product problem is not a feature. It is uncertainty.

Earlier work

The selected projects above represent the kind of work I do today, but the path here has included many different problems.

I have worked on financial products where confusion has real consequences, fashion and retail marketplaces, pension systems, developer communities, property intelligence, campaign websites, banking experiences, internal systems, and digital platforms.

I have worked with early stage teams where almost everything was still being figured out, and with established organisations where the challenge was changing something that already worked well enough to survive.

That variety has been useful.

It taught me to get comfortable with unfamiliar industries, ask better questions quickly, and understand that the right solution is often different from what the original brief suggests.

How I work

I usually start before the interface.

What is the organisation trying to do?

What are people struggling with?

What information is missing?

Where does the existing system break down?

What needs to change for the work to have a real effect?

From there, the work might involve research, strategy, product design, brand, technology, content, data, or some combination of them.

I do not expect every problem to need the same process.

The work should determine the method.

A broader view

The projects here come from different sectors, but there are a few things I keep coming back to.

Making complicated things clearer.

Making information useful.

Building trust into products and systems.

Helping organisations explain what they actually do.

Finding the problem underneath the brief.

And making things that people can use, not just things that look finished.

Those are the threads connecting the work.
