A calm base for everyday Barcelona

This bright home sits on a lived-in street in Gràcia, close to local bakeries, small squares and independent shops. The apartment has room to cook, work and slow down between days in the city.

Adriana shares a short neighbourhood guide with the places she uses herself. Check-in is arranged directly, so there is always a person to contact when you arrive.
