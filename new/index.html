Hello, I'm Tomi.

Human. Believer. African. Husband. Builder. Strategist.

I’m Nigerian, born in Lagos and rooted in a Yoruba family from Osun State. I’m curious about people, ideas, systems, and the ways they shape how we live. Faith is central to how I see the world, and so are the people, places, questions, and experiences that have shaped me.

This is my corner of the internet.

A place for the things I’ve made, the work I’ve done, the ideas I’m exploring, the things I’m learning, and the moments I want to remember.
