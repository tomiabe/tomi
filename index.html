My story, so far.

My full name is Jesutomilayo Abe. I was born in Lagos, into a Yoruba family from Osun State. I grew up in a home filled with love and faith, with room to explore and become my own person.

That curiosity took me to Computer Science at Covenant University, and later to a postgraduate programme in Creative Technology at the University of the West of England. I was drawn to both the logic of computing and the possibilities of making something with it.

Living in the UK widened my perspective. I began to notice how differently people organise things: their cities, institutions, creative work, and everyday life. It changed the questions I brought back with me.

Today, I’m married to Toro, whose faith and brilliance continually inspire me. We live on the outskirts of Lagos, just far enough to breathe. Home, faith, and the people in my life shape the decisions I make about work and everything around it.

I still follow a lot of interests. Art, design, technology, media, sustainability, culture, faith, and history all find their way into my reading and conversations. I like understanding how something works, why it matters, and whether it could work better.

What grounds me.

I’m a Christian. Faith is the lens through which I see my life, including the ordinary choices about how I spend my time and how I treat people.

I believe creativity is a gift to take seriously. Doing something well is a way of being grateful for it. That belief carries into how I build, learn, and serve other people.

Curiosity makes me ask questions. Conviction helps me decide what to do with the answers. I want to keep both, and stay willing to learn from experiences that challenge what I think I know.
