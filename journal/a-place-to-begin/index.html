A place to begin

For a long time, I treated my website like a problem to solve. I kept changing its shape because I could not quite fit everything I wanted to put there into a portfolio.

Work matters to me, but it is not the whole story. I also write, make things for the sake of learning, take photographs, and follow questions that have nothing to do with a client brief.

So this version is a home with rooms. You can get a sense of me from the front page, then spend time with whichever part interests you. The rooms can change as I do.

This journal is one of them. I want it to be somewhere I can leave a thought while it is still fresh, then return when I have more to say.
