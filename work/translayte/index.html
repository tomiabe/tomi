Translayte

A growth design engagement exploring how introducing free document translation could attract new customers and create clearer paths into paid translation, human review, and repeat use.

Period
2023 — 2024
Role
Growth Design Consultant
Focus
Growth strategy, product design, prototyping

The opportunity

Translayte already had a paid service for professional and certified translation. The opportunity was to add a lower commitment entry point that let prospective customers experience value before choosing the level of human support they needed.

The online document translation experience gave people a useful first interaction: upload a PDF, image, or Office file, receive a fast translation within the service limit, then decide what to do next.

The free experience became a new acquisition layer around the existing service. It introduced the value of Translayte's workflow, then guided customers toward more words, human review, or a recurring plan when the job required it.

One product, two reasons to pay

The growth model connected the free entry point to two distinct reasons to continue paying. One was capacity. The other was confidence.

01

Pay for more translation

When a document reached its included free allowance, customers could buy the remaining words or consider an annual plan for regular use.

02

Pay for human confidence

Once the automatic result was available, customers could add native proofreading, professional review, or specialist review based on how the document would be used.

Trust is part of conversion

These documents can affect travel, employment, education, legal matters, and family decisions. The interface needed to earn confidence before asking for payment.

Explain the next step. Progress messages describe what the system is checking and what the customer will receive.

Keep prices legible. Free usage, paid words, review costs, delivery timing, and billing terms stay visible near the decision.

Let work survive interruptions. Accounts, saved progress, and document history make a limit or delayed review feel recoverable.

Match the offer to the risk. A legal agreement can prompt specialist review without presenting every customer with the same generic promotion.

What shipped and what it enabled

The implementation added a free document translation entry point to Translayte's paid service and connected it to registration, progress, paid continuation, human review, and the document workspace. Each part made the next step clearer, easier to recover, and more relevant to the customer's document.

Activation

A useful first task gave prospective customers a reason to begin, while file guidance and a clear service limit made the entry point easier to understand.

Completion

Status updates and recoverable states carried customers from upload to result, protecting the work they had already started.

Conversion

Word top-ups, plans, and human review turned a completed free experience into clear paid choices matched to the customer's needs.

Order value

Document-aware review options made higher-value services easier to understand when accuracy, certification, or intended use raised the stakes.

Retention

Saved documents, credits, status, search, and filtering made the service useful beyond a single translation task.

The product lesson

The work connected free value, paid continuation, human confidence, and repeat use in one recoverable journey. Instead of asking every new customer to commit to a full translation order immediately, the experience let them begin with a useful task, understand the value of human expertise, and choose a paid service with more confidence. It created a more flexible growth path: immediate utility for acquisition, relevant upgrades for conversion, and saved work for return use.
