Translayte Content System

A creative direction engagement turning certified translation into a clearer, more human social story.

Period
2024
Role
Creative direction, visual design, content
Focus
Illustration, copywriting, social content, video

Make a specialist service easy to understand

Translation, certification, legalisation, and apostilles are important services, but they can be difficult to explain in a fast social feed. The work focused on turning those services into useful, memorable moments that could earn attention before asking for a quote.

Answer the question first

Each post started with a real customer question, then used plain language and a clear example to make the service feel less intimidating.

Earn attention quickly

The selected illustration direction gave the content a recognisable world, while stronger openings made the first moment easier to notice.

Leave a next step

Useful education led naturally to a free quote, a service explanation, or a prompt to prepare the right document.

A visual language for confidence

The system balanced authority with warmth. Translayte's brand colours stayed visible, while editorial illustration and direct copy made the subject easier to approach.

Brand colour with room to breathe

Deep blue carried trust and structure. Cream and coral softened the subject. Gold marked important words and calls to action.

Aa Bb Cc

Practical typography keeps the system clear

A clean sans-serif gave the campaign a practical typographic voice: easy to read at speed, flexible across formats, and direct without feeling cold.

Illustration over stock imagery

A human, editorial illustration style gave the campaign a consistent point of view across people, places, documents, and abstract ideas.

Copy that teaches and moves

Headlines carried the question. Supporting copy handled the explanation. The final line made the next action clear.

One system, many formats

The same visual logic moved between single posts, educational sequences, campaign sets, and short video.

A campaign system in practice

The content was organised as connected story sets rather than a loose collection of posts. Each set started with a familiar customer concern, built understanding in stages, and ended with a useful reason to take the next step.

Motion made the explanation easier to enter

These two short pieces extended the same educational system into motion, helping people understand when certified documents need more than a standard translation.

From direction to delivery

The value was not only in the finished posts. It was in shaping a usable creative direction, producing a repeatable content rhythm, and responding to feedback without losing the system.

01

Set the direction

Two illustration approaches made the creative decision tangible. The selected direction became the basis for the campaign.

02

Build the content

Weekly sets turned the brief into a working mix of educational posts, campaign messages, and short video.

03

Refine in context

Client feedback improved tone, representation, logo placement, and the relationship between copy and image.

The creative lesson

Good content direction makes a complex service feel easier to enter. The campaign gave Translayte a warmer visual world, a clearer educational voice, and a practical system that could keep producing useful stories.
