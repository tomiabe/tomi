Period

Role

Focus

A marketplace where clarity earns trust

Eze connected buyers and sellers of wholesale electronics across markets. Buyers had to understand device condition, stock, pricing, payment, and delivery before committing to orders with meaningful commercial value.

Inventory needs context

Bulk stock becomes useful when the model, grade, carrier, storage, price, and available quantity are easy to scan and compare.

Market data needs a decision

Seller asks and buyer bids had to explain the available market, then guide a buyer toward purchase or a Buy Request.

Orders need a visible path

Quantity changes, delivery addresses, payment, and order status needed to stay clear throughout the purchase and fulfilment flow.

One system across product and brand

I built and led Eze's growing design function across product and brand, creating a shared language for a team of four. The system brought identity, interface patterns, and product decisions into the same working structure.

01

Identity

A visual identity shaped around exchange gave the marketplace a clear and credible presence.

02

Components

Reusable inputs, controls, status, tables, and feedback patterns made complex flows easier to build and learn.

03

Workflows

Shared patterns connected product search, market context, checkout, and buyer account activity.

A buyer journey built around the next decision

Working directly with a designer, I translated buyer feedback gathered by sales into clearer search, pricing, and purchase decisions. Each screen keeps the relevant information and action close together.

01

Source products in bulk

Category navigation, filters, sorting, grid and list views help a buyer narrow a broad inventory without losing the core signals that drive a purchase.

  • Filter by model, grade, carrier, storage, and price.
  • Switch between visual scanning and denser list comparison.
  • Create a Buy Request when the needed stock is not available.
02

Read the market before committing

The product page puts bulk availability, the best seller ask, buyer demand, and a clear bid action in one place. Buyers can purchase immediately or set the maximum price they are prepared to pay.

  • Make the relationship between seller asks and buyer bids legible.
  • Keep grade, configuration, condition, and fulfilment details close to the purchase decision.
  • Give unavailable or unsuitable demand a clear alternative through Buy Requests.

Make the purchase recoverable

I partnered with product on prioritisation and engineering on implementation details, using sales feedback to focus the checkout on the points where buyers hesitated. The experience keeps the order visible as the buyer moves through each step.

A clearer checkout path

A focused checkout initiative looked at where buyers hesitated before committing to an order. The work clarified quantities, delivery, payment, and the sequence of steps needed to complete a purchase.

Research signals

Sales conversations and journey data showed where buyers needed more certainty before placing an order.

Checkout focus

Quantities, delivery details, payment choices, and an unclear sequence became the core areas to improve.

Tested changes

High-fidelity flows helped refine validation, recovery, and the information needed at each decision.

Stronger completion

Internal signals after release showed a healthier path from cart to completed order.

Lower friction

The revised experience removed common reasons to leave an order unfinished.

Design made the marketplace easier to trust

As design lead, I connected a visual identity, a shared component language, and the buyer journey into one product system with design, product, engineering, and sales. The result gave buyers a clearer way to evaluate stock, understand the market, complete an order, and return to the work they had already started. It became the foundation that has since supported Eze's sale of more than 500,000 devices.
