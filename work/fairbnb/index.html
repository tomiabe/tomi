Fairbnb

Bringing fair stays, local experiences, and community benefit into one trip people can shape for themselves.

Period
2022 — 2023
Role
Brand & Product Design Consultant
Focus
Guest journey, platform integration, interaction design, design system

How can a trip feel whole when its best reasons to exist live in separate places?

Fairbnb had more than a place to stay. It had a cooperative model, locally rooted experiences, and community projects that could benefit from each booking. The work was to make those ideas legible as one guest decision, without forcing a traveller through separate products.

That meant treating a destination as the organising principle. A guest could arrive with one intention, then choose how much of the trip to build around it.

A guest should build a trip, not reconcile separate bookings.

The Trip Builder is the connective tissue of the platform. It lets a stay and an experience relate to the same dates, the same group, and the same destination. It also gives the selected community project a meaningful place beside the practical details of booking.

  1. 01 / ChooseAdd a stay or an experience from a shared search context.
  2. 02 / ComposeKeep the details of both choices together as the trip takes shape.
  3. 03 / CommitReview the booking and its local contribution in one transaction.
  4. 04 / ReturnManage the confirmed trip, messages, and project context in one place.

A cooperative idea made concrete through the guest journey.

The work gave Fairbnb a validated product direction, unifying stays, community-funded projects, and local experiences into one seamless booking experience.

Testing with hosts and conscious travelers confirmed that treating destinations as the organizing principle removed multi-cart friction, while making the 50/50 community revenue allocation immediately clear at checkout.
