Studio

Tomi Abe Studio is my independent creative technology practice. It is where I work with clients, build ideas of my own, and make room to learn by doing.

The practice has changed shape since 2013. Design, technology, research and media have all had a place in it. What has stayed constant is my interest in making complex things clearer and more useful to people.
